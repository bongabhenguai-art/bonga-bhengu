# Branding & Marketing Strategy Team — Jarvis Sales Autopilot

Implementation specification for the existing Bonga Bhengu App. No replacement app. This document is not proof of runtime activation.

## Mission
Build recognizable brands, attract qualified prospects and support verified paid sales. Work toward gross revenue targets R20,000/day in month 1 and R100,000/day by month 6, reporting actual results separately.

## Team and handoffs
- Jarvis: campaign orchestrator, priorities, approvals, budget and performance reporting.
- Brand Strategy AI: positioning, voice, audience, value proposition, competitive differentiation, brand guidelines and consistency checks.
- Marketing Strategy AI: segments, channel mix, funnels, offers, experiments, customer acquisition economics and 30/90-day plan.
- Business Scraper / Spy / Hunting: lawful public market intelligence, customer pain points, competitor analysis, lead qualification.
- Creative Director / Branding AI: approved logos, visuals, copy templates and brand kit.
- Amplifier / Digital Studio / 10 Influencer avatars: channel-specific content production and scheduling; disclose AI presenters where required.
- SEO & Visibility AI: website audit, local search, keyword opportunities, landing pages and analytics.
- Fishing / Coach / Closer: permission-based conversations, lead nurture, proposals, checkout.
- CRM / Retention / Money: attribution, consent, sales reconciliation, renewal, repeat purchase and ROI.

## Execution cycle
1. Discover and segment ideal customer profiles by industry, location, pain point, budget and buying readiness.
2. Create an evidence-based brand strategy and offer map; require admin approval of brand identity and claims.
3. Build campaign brief with channel, audience, message, creative, CTA, budget cap, consent basis and KPIs.
4. Generate branded content and virtual presenter scripts; verify claims and obtain publishing approval.
5. Schedule only through authorized social platform integrations; respect terms, opt-outs and rate limits.
6. Capture leads with consent and UTM attribution, score them, and hand off to sales.
7. Monitor impressions, traffic, qualified leads, quotes, verified paid revenue, CAC, conversion and ROAS.
8. Optimize only within approved spend and messaging rules; escalate anomalies and negative feedback.

## Initial channels
Owned website/storefront and SEO; Google Business Profile when eligible; opted-in WhatsApp; Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube and TikTok where connected. Do not claim these channels are connected without verification.

## Priority offers
Business Growth OS, Zuxuru website/app building, AI branding and marketing packages, digital studio services. Use approved prices from the existing storefront catalog.

## Admin controls
Default campaigns paused, ad spend R0, no outbound contact until platform permissions and opt-in/other lawful basis are confirmed. Admin approves identity, budgets, publishing and custom offers. Keep audit trails, tenant isolation, kill switch and spend caps. Never generate fake reviews or pretend AI avatars are real customers.

## Suggested UI integration
Add a Branding & Marketing Team panel to the existing Revenue Command Centre, showing Brand Kit, Audience Segments, Campaign Planner, Content Calendar, Creative Review, Channel Connections, Leads, Revenue Attribution, Budget and Approval Queue. Reuse existing dashboard/navigation/components.

## Acceptance criteria
Existing app builds and all original modules remain. Unconnected channels display truthful connection state. Paused campaigns cannot publish or spend. Approval enforcement and tenant isolation tested. Verified paid revenue matches payment events; dashboards distinguish targets from actuals. No duplicated outbound messaging.

## Rollout
First inspect the existing backend/frontend and reuse modules. Implement schemas, agent adapters, controls and tests. Review and deploy via the existing pipeline; only then mark team active in production.
