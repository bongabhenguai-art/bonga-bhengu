# Lead Generation, Hooks & Growth Loops Team

Integrate into the existing Bonga Bhengu App, coordinated by Jarvis and the current Branding, Marketing Strategy, Sales Autopilot, CRM and Digital Studio teams. Do not create another app.

## Mission
Turn evidence-based customer problems into useful hooks, permission-based leads, paid orders, repeat business and referrals. Revenue targets: R20,000/day Month 1, R100,000/day Month 6; not guarantees.

## Employees
Jarvis (orchestration); Lead Hunter (public demand signals); Customer Problem Analyst (pain point evidence); Hook Creator (headline, opening, CTA variations); Content Strategist (channel content); Lead Magnet Builder (audit/checklist); Funnel Builder (landing pages, opt-in forms, UTM); Growth Loop Designer (content, referral, retention); Fishing AI (nurture); Closer AI (approved offers); Analytics AI (attribution, conversion and experiments). Reuse existing agents where capabilities overlap.

## Pipeline
Public research and opted-in inbound signals -> customer segments and needs -> hook variants and useful content -> approved publication -> landing page/lead magnet -> consented CRM capture -> qualification -> personalized offer -> secure checkout -> verified paid order -> fulfillment -> retention and voluntary referral.

## Three loops
1. Content: discover questions -> create useful content -> publish -> analyze qualified visits -> improve.
2. Referral: successful delivery -> satisfaction check -> optional referral invitation -> new opted-in lead -> fulfilled service.
3. Retention: completed service -> customer success -> relevant needs -> permitted follow-up -> renewal or upsell.

## Example hook templates
- Business Growth OS: 'Your business may be losing customers online without knowing why.' CTA: Request a visibility assessment.
- Zuxuru: 'Still running your business entirely through WhatsApp? Give customers a proper place to discover and buy.' CTA: Explore website options.
- AI Branding: 'Customers cannot choose your business if they cannot understand what makes it different.' CTA: Request a brand review.

## Dashboard
Visitors; hook CTR; landing conversion; consented leads; qualified leads; quotes; paid orders; verified gross/net revenue; conversion; CAC; ROAS; referral rate; repeat purchase rate; unsubscribe rate. Segment by campaign, channel, offer and tenant. Distinguish goals from actuals.

## Controls
Default paused; zero advertising spend without explicit authorization; publishing only to authorized accounts; no unsolicited mass messaging; consent and opt-out enforcement; no fake testimonials, false urgency or fabricated results. Brand and claims approval, role permissions, tenant isolation, rate limits, audit trail, kill switch and payment-webhook verification.

## Integration checklist
Inspect existing agents, storefront, CRM and data model. Reuse routes and components; implement adapters and admin controls, then tests for lead deduplication, attribution, consent, opt-outs, approval gates, budget caps and tenant separation. Deploy through existing pipeline only after review. This specification does not itself activate production.
