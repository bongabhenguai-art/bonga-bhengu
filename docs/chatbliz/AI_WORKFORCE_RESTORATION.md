# CHATBLIZ OS — Full AI Workforce and Module Restoration Directive

**Architecture invariant:** Chatbliz OS has exactly FOUR customer-facing core service products: My Brand, My Website, My Content, My Business. These are product packaging, **not a limit on modules, agents, integrations, engines or features**.

## Restore, audit, unify
Inventory every prior Bonga Bhengu/Zuxuru/Fukulisane business-oriented AI employee, module, workflow, MCP connector, tool, skill and repository dependency. Categorize as **working / broken / duplicate / missing / external integration / unsafe or unsupported**. Reuse and repair existing source before replacement. Do not claim that an agent works until tested. Do not overwrite the private fashion-only Bonga Bhengu OS or its live site.

## AI workforce inventory to reconcile against actual source
- **Command / operations:** Jarvis coordinator, Assistant, Workflow, Fixer, Problem Resolver, User Engineer, Illustration & Architecture, Researcher, Storage/Library, Third-Party Connector, AI Business Coach, AI Mentor.
- **Discovery and sales:** Business Scraper (evidence-backed business problems, not merely contacts), Hunter, Spy, Trend/Product Hunter, Fishing, Closer, Negotiation/Listening Assistant, Business Converter, Lead Qualification, CRM, Sales Engine, Growth Sales Engine, Appointment Scheduler, Follow-up, Customer Retention.
- **Brand / growth:** Branding Agent, Marketing Strategy, Business Visibility Intelligence, Business Connection Intelligence, Business Growth Intelligence, Execution Intelligence, Reputation Manager, SEO, Campaign/Advertising, AI Influencer Avatars, Amplifier, Social Publishing.
- **Creation / development:** Zuxuru AI App & Website Builder, WebBuilderBot, Coder, DesignForge, Template Builder, Digital Studio, Video/Podcast/Livestream, Media Editing, Fashion as optional customer industry template, Education/Academy/LMS tools where compatible.
- **Commerce / administration:** Money Agent, Payments/Wallet, Quotes, Orders, Tax reporting, Analytics, Sourcing/White Label/POD, Subscription/Billing, Admin Governance, Customer Support.
- **Platform:** MCP client/tool registry, model router, permissions, shared business profile, multi-tenant CRM, messaging/WhatsApp, file storage, job scheduler, logging, approvals, audit trail, connector management.

This list is a **discovery checklist**, not a claim that each agent exists in the code. Find actual implementations and map them to one or more products and to cross-cutting services.

## Product mapping rules
- **My Brand:** identity, research, branding, reputation, positioning, visibility.
- **My Website:** app/site builder, code, templates, publishing, SEO, integrations.
- **My Content:** studio, images/video/audio, podcasts/livestreams, influencers, campaigns, scheduling.
- **My Business:** lead intelligence, sales team, CRM, appointments, negotiation assistance, conversion, orders, money and analytics.
- **Shared across all:** Jarvis, Fixer, MCP, approvals, storage, business profile, WhatsApp Business, authorization, billing, logs, workflow engine.

## Chatboard-first workflow
Customer request → intent and package check → Jarvis plan → MCP tool selection → authorized specialist agents → real execution → previews/results inside same chatboard → customer approval for external actions → persistence, reporting and follow-up. Visual editors are embedded workspaces, not new top-level products.

## WhatsApp and sales safeguards
Use authorized WhatsApp Business Platform APIs, customer consent, approved templates, webhook validation and opt-out. No scraping private numbers, unsolicited mass marketing or unauthorized auto-sending. AI can draft, qualify, coach and schedule approved follow-ups; it must not fabricate prospects or sales.

## Agile implementation
**Sprint 0:** source audit and restoration matrix; find actual existing modules and owners.
**Sprint 1:** shared chatboard, Jarvis orchestration, MCP registry, auth, four product entitlements.
**Sprint 2:** reconnect high-value sales team (Hunter/Fishing/Closer/Fixer), CRM and approval workflow.
**Sprint 3:** reconnect brand, builder, content studio and social publishing.
**Sprint 4:** WhatsApp integration and end-to-end business growth workflow.
**Sprint 5:** monitoring, regression tests, tenant isolation, accessibility, staging and release readiness.

## Acceptance criteria
1. No original business capability is silently omitted; each has a mapped status and migration decision.
2. Exactly four customer-facing core product packages, without sacrificing the shared workforce.
3. Real end-to-end tool execution, state persistence and transparent failure reporting.
4. Reusable cross-package data with tenant boundaries, permissions and customer approvals.
5. Existing live Bonga Bhengu OS remains untouched pending separate fashion-only work.
6. Codex reports implemented files, test evidence, blockers and next steps. No fake success.
