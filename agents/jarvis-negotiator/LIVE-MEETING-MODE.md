# Jarvis — Live Customer Negotiation Copilot

Extend the existing Bonga Bhengu OS Digital Studio and Sales Team; do not create a duplicate application.

## Meeting mode
A user-initiated "Start Jarvis Negotiation" control in seller dashboard / Studio. Support phone microphone or typed conversation notes, and optional discreet earphone spoken coaching. Display a large mobile-friendly live transcript and concise suggested next response, objection-handling, package comparison, pricing calculator, meeting notes and follow-up. Use streaming transcription only where a working authorized provider exists; show unavailable/error state otherwise.

## Live workflow
1. Select business customer and meeting type (in-person, phone, WhatsApp call); obtain any legally required consent before recording/transcribing and clearly indicate microphone status.
2. Jarvis listens to authorized conversation or reads notes, identifies the prospect's business problem, budget, urgency, buying authority and next step without assuming intent.
3. Ask the Hunting/Fishing, Design Influence, Fixer, Closer and Money agents for verified relevant offers and pricing. Recommend an empathetic response in real time; do not impersonate the customer or speak without the seller's approval.
4. Present at most 3 concise response options: discovery question, value proposition, or objection answer. Respect published prices, discounts and margin/approval limits. Never invent scarcity, testimonials, results or guaranteed returns.
5. With user approval, prepare quote, checkout link, CRM record and mutually confirmed appointment or follow-up. Do not auto-send messages or collect card details in transcript.
6. Show verified outcome: quote sent, meeting booked, payment confirmed or pending. No fabricated closed deals.

## Implementation acceptance
Mobile first; minimal UI latency; accessible captions and optional voice output; consent gate; pause/mute/stop and delete-session controls; tenant-scoped CRM; privacy-safe retention; secure tokens; clear human approval before offers, messages, payment, recording and account changes; end-to-end tests on Android and in person.

## Integration
Reuse existing Studio mic/camera pipeline and sales orchestration; route speech-to-text, response generation and text-to-speech via authorized centrally governed services. CodeRabbit review, Fixer audit and deployment verification required.
