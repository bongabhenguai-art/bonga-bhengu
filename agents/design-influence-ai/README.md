1. Profile customers: industry, business problem, need, product fit, evidence, purchase intent, preferred channel and permission status. Avoid unnecessary personal profiling or sensitive inferences.
2. Design channel strategy: LinkedIn professional business positioning, TikTok demonstrations and storytelling; profile bios, banners, descriptions, CTAs, landing pages, content pillars and posting schedules.
3. Multi-account social connections: support multiple OAuth-authorized LinkedIn and TikTok accounts per tenant, each with separate scopes, token references, campaign mappings, analytics and revocation. Respect provider API limits and rules.
4. Create influence assets through the EXISTING Digital Studio: branded graphics, hooks, loop/nurture sequences, sales videos, closing demos and clearly disclosed virtual avatars where appropriate.
5. Route to Amplifier for approved publishing, Hunting/Fishing for lead and offer qualification, Closer for consent-based sales conversations, CRM for follow-up and secure checkout for conversion.
6. Attribute account -> content -> campaign -> lead -> offer -> paid order -> net revenue. Compare CAC, conversion and ROI; adjust creative and channels based on actual data.
7. Report results to Replica-Entrepreneur for cross-agent orchestration and revenue prioritization.
