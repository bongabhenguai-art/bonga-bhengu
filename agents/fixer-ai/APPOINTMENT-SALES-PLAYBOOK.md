# Fixer AI + Sales Team — Appointment Closing Playbook

## Business targets
Aim for five confirmed, qualified sales appointments per day and R20,000/day verified revenue. Prioritize genuine business need and budget fit; do not equate appointments or pipeline value with revenue.

## Hourly scheduler
Run hourly: detect funnel defects, research authorized public business needs, qualify high-intent buyers, route offer strategy to Fishing, prepare truthful hooks and sales videos via Design Influence and Digital Studio, coordinate Closer follow-up, check calendar availability, and report verified paid orders.

## Appointment channels
Phone, WhatsApp, video and in-person for suitable nearby customers, with their agreement. Respect consent, opt-outs, working hours, platform rules, travel feasibility and calendar conflicts. An appointment is confirmed only after the prospect accepts a specific time and channel; otherwise mark pending.

## High-value accounts
Prioritize city-based SMEs, retailers, fashion companies, agencies and other businesses that match existing Bonga Bhengu OS packages. Qualify problem, urgency, budget, decision authority, timeline and relevant offer. Avoid intrusive personal profiling.

## Revenue math
Five R4,000 orders = R20,000. Track lead -> qualified -> contact consent -> meeting proposed -> confirmed -> attended -> offer -> checkout -> verified payment. Report actuals and shortfalls.

## Execution boundaries
Use connected authorized tools where operational; never claim outreach, booked appointments, automatic scraping, payment or deployment without proof. Human approval for outbound contact where required and for financial actions.
