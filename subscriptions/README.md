# Bonga Bhengu subscription products

One shared Business Master File and tenant identity serves four separately selectable products:
1. Website Builder & Hosting
2. Digital Visibility
3. Digital Creative Studio
4. Digital Banner

Packages: Choose Any One R299/month; Choose Any Two R499/month; Choose Any Three price TBD; All-in-One (four) price TBD. Previous three-product All-in-One R699/month is historical and must not be silently transferred.

## Plugin and configuration architecture
Each product declares capabilities and required connections; the central Connection Wizard must verify official OAuth or other authorized integrations. Tenant-scoped permissions and a confirmed paid subscription gate access to production execution.

- Website Builder & Hosting: domain/hosting provider, CMS, site publisher.
- Digital Visibility: Search Console, analytics, business profiles and social read permissions.
- Digital Creative Studio: authorized image/video AI providers, local/cloud render workers and storage.
- Digital Banner: campaign manager, banner inventory, approved publisher and analytics.

No OAuth or payment credentials are included in this repository. Catalog validation alone does not activate billing, verify payments, authenticate users, connect providers or deploy services.
