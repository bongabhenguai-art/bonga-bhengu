# AI Business Intelligence Platform Solution

## Vision

Build a connector-first SaaS that discovers, verifies, diagnoses,
improves, and grows a business using evidence-backed data.

## Core Architecture

1.  Discovery Engine
2.  Verification Engine
3.  Business Knowledge Graph
4.  Connector Hub
5.  Intelligence Modules
6.  Wizard Engine
7.  Automation Engine
8.  Dashboard & Reporting

## Discovery

Inputs: - Business name - Founder - Website (optional) - Industry -
Location

Outputs: - Business profile - Digital assets - Confidence scores -
Missing information

## Verification

Each discovered fact stores: - Source - Confidence (0--100) - Last
verified - Status (Verified / Likely / Unverified)

## Connector Hub

Identity: - Business registries - Google Business Profile

Marketing: - Google Analytics - Google Search Console - Meta -
LinkedIn - X - TikTok - YouTube

CRM: - HubSpot - Zoho - Salesforce - GoHighLevel

Commerce: - Shopify - WooCommerce - Stripe

Communication: - WhatsApp Business - Email - SMS
