# C5 — CONSCIENTIOUS

The platform should be responsible, trustworthy, and transparent.

### Data Integrity

-  Identify the source of business data. 
-  Show synchronization status. 
-  Timestamp important records. 
-  Protect customer information. 
-  Preserve audit history. 

### AI Principles

AI should:

-  Assist with drafting and analysis. 
-  Explain recommendations when practical. 
-  Respect user permissions. 
-  Clearly distinguish recommendations from facts. 
-  Escalate important decisions for human approval. 

### Automation Principles

Automate routine work such as:

-  Scheduling reminders. 
-  Creating follow-up tasks. 
-  Drafting content. 
-  Monitoring connected services. 

Require approval for actions with legal, financial, or contractual consequences unless the owner has explicitly configured otherwise.
