# Zuxuru Business Visibility Station — Builder Specification

## Product entry
Replace “Enter your business name to check your visibility” with:

**Set up your business**

Business Name → DeepSearch → Discover Business → Review Identity/Registration → Sign up/Login → Save Business Profile → Business Visibility Station.

## Canonical Business Profile
Persist:
- business/legal name
- registration/verification
- founder/owner
- industry
- history/founded date
- locations
- contacts
- website
- social media
- products/services
- reviews
- directories
- maps/listings
- discovered platform profiles
- visibility scores
- evidence
- gaps
- opportunities
- connections/integrations
- content/assets
- growth activity
- complete chronological history

## Nothing goes to waste
Every DeepSearch finding becomes one of:
1. accepted profile data
2. evidence
3. platform profile
4. missing/not-visible record
5. connection task
6. visibility gap
7. growth opportunity
8. history event

The permanent mini-dashboard/command center:
- Business Profile
- Business Builder
- Visibility & Score
- Platform Profiles
- Connections / CIA
- Digital Studio
- Marketing & Growth
- Leads / Inbox
- AI Autopilot
- Analytics
- History
