Add spy layer

A "spy layer" is only appropriate if it focuses on ethical competitive intelligence and business monitoring. It should never collect private, confidential, or unauthorized data from competitors or other businesses.

A good replacement is an AI Competitive Intelligence & Market Monitoring Layer.

Important Design Principle

The intelligence layer should only use information that is public, provided by the business, or shared through authorized integrations. It should not attempt to access confidential competitor data, private accounts, or protected systems. This keeps the platform trustworthy, legally compliant, and valuable for businesses.
