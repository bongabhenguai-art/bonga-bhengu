# Connected Enterprise Intelligence Architecture

## Core Principle

Every Intelligence Workplace must be connected to live business modules,
real databases, approved third-party integrations, and AI engines. No
workplace should operate on placeholder information or isolated
dashboards.

Every recommendation, report, analysis, workflow, and automation must be
traceable to real data or clearly identified as an estimate based on
public information.

# Direct Connect Wizard

Every module includes a Direct Connect Wizard so customers can securely
authorize their own accounts.

## Examples

-   Website
-   Google Business Profile
-   Google Analytics
-   Google Search Console
-   Facebook
-   Instagram
-   LinkedIn
-   TikTok
-   YouTube
-   X
-   WhatsApp Business
-   Microsoft 365
-   Google Workspace
-   HubSpot
-   Salesforce
-   Stripe
-   QuickBooks
-   Xero

## The wizard should

-   Explain why the connection is useful.
-   Guide the user through sign-in and authorization.
-   Confirm connection status.
-   Test data access.
-   Display the date of the last successful sync.
-   Allow reconnection or removal.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Live Data Requirement

Every screen must display the source of its information.

Each metric should indicate whether it comes from: - Publicly available
information. - A customer-authorized connected account. - Internal
business records. - AI-generated estimates.

This transparency helps users understand the confidence and origin of
every insight.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Final Principle

Every dashboard, score, recommendation, and AI action must be linked to
one or more live modules. If a required module is not connected or data
is unavailable, the platform should identify the missing dependency,
explain how to connect it, and avoid presenting incomplete or misleading
results.
