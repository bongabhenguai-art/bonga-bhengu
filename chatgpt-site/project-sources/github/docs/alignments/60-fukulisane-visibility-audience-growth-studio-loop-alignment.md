## Distinctive source principle
**Visibility comes first; connection comes second where required; Digital Studio is the improvement engine.** The source's key business goal is not merely content generation but **Digital Visibility → Digital Connection → Audience Growth → Business Growth**.

### Customer-to-outcome loop
1. Public **Visibility Audit** discovers the business's current discoverability and evidence-backed gaps.
2. **Visibility findings / score** show evidence, uncertainty, and where improvement is needed. Example scores in the source are illustrative, never measured facts.
3. **Package / offer** matches an actual customer need and the Bonga subscription/product entitlement.
4. **Customer chooses service**; where an external owned platform is needed, the user authorizes it through the existing Connection Wizard. Built-in capabilities must not require technical setup.
5. **Creative Studio** converts diagnosed gaps into appropriate real content: educational clips, interviews, customer stories, testimonials, podcasts, livestreams, events, product demonstrations, brand videos and website content.
6. **AI Employees** repurpose verified original assets into platform-appropriate outputs, subject to rights, consent, quality review and approval.
7. **Execution** distributes content through authorized channels and records provider receipts, URLs and timestamps.
8. **Analytics / Learning** measure actual reach, watch time, engagement, clicks, leads and conversions where data is available; compare before/after and recommend next actions.
9. **Autopilot** recommends another Studio brief and re-audit. External actions remain within consent, policy and admin controls.

## Specific content-repurposing contract
The source illustrates one real customer livestream producing a full recording, podcast episode, five short videos, quote graphic, website article, FAQ, social posts, marketing asset and email content. **These are potential variants, not automatically completed assets.** Every derivative must have a real file ID, parent source ID, approved usage rights, format, destination and processing status. The number of clips depends on content quality, length and plan limits.
