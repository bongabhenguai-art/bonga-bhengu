# Chatbliz OS paid packages — proposal
Commercial decision: no permanent free customer tier. Offer a controlled test drive, then paid subscriptions. The following prices are proposed, not approved or live.

| Tier | Proposed ZAR/month | Main offer |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Starter | R149 | Jarvis, guided brand, website draft, basic content and business planning |
| Professional | R399 | Starter plus publishing workflow, content calendar, lead management, Coach, Adviser and limited Studio |
| Business | R899 | Professional plus Hunting/Fishing/Closer sales agents, campaigns, analytics, team seats and Studio production |
| Enterprise | Quote | Business plus custom integrations, governance, support and negotiated usage |

The four customer-facing services remain My Brand, My Website, My Content and My Business. These are packages, not separate apps. Open-source software can power paid customer services. Studio tools must respect licenses.

Test drive: limited real workflow producing a brand concept, website preview or short media conversion; disclose quotas, watermark and export restrictions; no automatic paid enrollment or billing; show actual status. Separate demo data, rate-limit usage and verify ownership.

Codex implementation sequence: inspect existing subscriptions and checkout; confirm prices and VAT with owner; add central package catalog and server-side entitlements; add persistent demo usage tracking; implement comparison and upgrade flow inside existing shell; connect payment provider only after authorization; test access, usage limits, tenant separation, billing consent and cancellation; verify deployment. Do not replace existing app or subscriptions.

Status: requirements only. Not implemented, charged or deployed.
