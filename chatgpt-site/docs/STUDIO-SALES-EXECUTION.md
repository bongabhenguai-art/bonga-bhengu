# Digital Studio as the Sales Content Engine

Extend the EXISTING Bonga Bhengu Digital Studio. Do not create a second studio or replacement application.

## Agent workflow
Replica-Entrepreneur coordinates Business Scraper, Spy, Hunting, Fishing, Branding, Amplifier, Closer, CRM and Studio employees. For each campaign, derive a real customer need and offer; generate a brief, script, storyboard, approved assets and a measurable CTA; produce hook, nurture-loop, closer, product/sales, approved influencer-avatar and retargeting videos using existing Studio capabilities.

## Publishing
Support multiple independently authorized LinkedIn and TikTok accounts per tenant, official provider permissions, account selection, per-account campaigns, tracking URLs, consent and opt-out rules. Where APIs do not support publishing or messaging, provide manual handoff. Never claim posts were published without provider confirmation.

## Sales measurement
Attribute creative -> account -> campaign -> lead -> qualified lead -> checkout -> verified paid order -> revenue. Track views, watch-through, CTR, lead rate, conversion rate, net revenue and creative costs. Optimize toward R5,000–R20,000/day as a business target, not a guarantee.

## Production modes
Reuse camera/phone capture, editing, recording, podcast, livestream, AI avatar/video and existing media pipelines where operational. Mark unavailable tools accurately; never invent generated video assets or publish unapproved likenesses.

## Review and release
Replica-Entrepreneur audits funnel economics and content relevance. CodeRabbit reviews actual code changes when connected. Require tests, authorization, budget caps, payment verification and explicit approval for production release.
